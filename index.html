Cry Wolf

Huge fiend (monstrosity), chaotic evil
Armor Class 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points 210 (20d12 + 80)
Speed 50 ft., climb 30 ft.
STR 22 (+6)
DEX 16 (+3)
CON 20 (+5)
INT 8 (-1)
WIS 14 (+2)
CHA 18 (+4)
Saving Throws Str +10, Con +9, Wis +6
Skills Athletics +10, Perception +6, Stealth +7
Damage Resistances cold, fire, necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
Damage Immunities poison
Condition Immunities charmed, frightened, poisoned
Senses truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
Languages Abyssal, telepathy 120 ft.
Challenge 12 (8,400 XP) • Proficiency Bonus +4

Abyssal Void. The wolf cannot be slain so long as hunger gnaws within its hollow chest. When reduced to 0 hit points, it enters a catatonic frenzy until a fresh organ offering is made.

Blood Frenzy. The wolf has advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its hit points.

Keen Hearing and Smell. The wolf has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell, and tracks the adventurer's cursor across planar boundaries.

ACTIONS

Multiattack. Cry Wolf makes three attacks: one with its Bite and two with its Claws.

Abyssal Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage. If the target is an organ offering, it is instantly consumed.

Sorrowful Howl (Recharge 5–6). The wolf emits a mournful wail that echoes through the cavern. Each creature within 60 ft. must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute.

LEGENDARY ACTIONS

The wolf can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

Stalk Cursor. The wolf moves up to half its speed directly toward the adventurer's cursor.

Snap Jaws (Costs 2 Actions). The wolf snaps viciously, producing an arterial gore splatter within 5 feet.
