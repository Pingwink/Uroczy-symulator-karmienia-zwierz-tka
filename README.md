# 🐺 CRY WOLF — D&D 5e Abyssal 3D Encounter

An atmospheric, interactive 3D Tabletop RPG horror encounter built with **Three.js**, **WebGL**, and **Web Audio API**.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Cast Offering**: `[Left Click]` or `[Space]` drops the selected anatomical organ onto the cavern altar.
2. **Switch Relics**: Keys `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`, `[4]` equip different components:
   - `[1]` **Heart of the Martyr** (Necromancy)
   - `[2]` **Cerebral Relic** (Divination)
   - `[3]` **Weeping Eye** (Evocation)
   - `[4]` **Coiled Entrails** (Conjuration)
3. **Roll D20**: Press `[D]` or click **🎲 Roll D20** to trigger a skill check.
4. **Inspect Monster Sheet**: Press `[B]` or click **📜 Stat Block** to view Cry Wolf's D&D 5e Monster Manual sheet.
5. **Camera Controls**:
   - `[Right Click + Drag]`: Orbit around the beast.
   - `[C]`: Toggle Cinematic Scrying Follow Camera.
   - `[R]`: Recenter view.
6. **Sound**: Press `[M]` to toggle synthesized ambient audio and bardsong.

---

## 🛠️ Technology
- **Three.js** (WebGL 3D Rendering & PCF Shadows)
- **FBXLoader** (Hardware skinning & animations)
- **Web Audio API** (Procedural audio synthesis — heartbeats, drones, footsteps, dice rolls, crunch bites)
- **Vanilla JavaScript & CSS** (No external frameworks or build steps required)
